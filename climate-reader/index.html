What the chart says

Each strategy is counted on its own, so the percentages overlap. Zone boundaries are approximate, after the Givoni and Milne building bioclimatic chart (Lechner, Heating, Cooling, Lighting, fig 5.10). Thermal mass zones only count days with a daily swing of 10 °C (18 °F) or more.
